Tornfolio

A Torn property helper that keeps your homes, tenants, dates, rent prices, and selling ideas in one clear place.

Under development Join Discord

At a glance

  • See each home, who uses it, who pays for it, and when it ends
  • Keep your homes, partner homes, and shared homes tidy
  • Get simple rent or sale ideas for homes you own

Status

Tornfolio is under development and is not public yet.

The public download is hidden until testing is finished. Private testing will be shared through Discord when it is ready.

Access

Tornfolio uses its own TF pass. It is separate from Pit Guru, Custom Race Filter, and the other tools.

The Settings screen tells you whether the Torn user from your saved key has active Tornfolio access.

What this tool does

Leases

Shows homes, owners, renters, prices, dates, days left, status, and notes.

Fetching

Can fill your homes, the home you live in, and partner homes when Torn has that information available.

Suggestions

Can suggest whether your own homes may be better for rent or sale, with a rental length when useful.

Easy editing

Fetched values stay fixed, calculated values update on their own, and manual entries are kept simple.

Why it is still private

The tool is being cleaned up before release so it is easier to read, does not show duplicate homes, and does not jump around while you edit the ledger.

Price checks happen quietly in the background. You should see the useful answer, not a noisy wall of scan results.
